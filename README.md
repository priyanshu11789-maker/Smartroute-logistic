AI-Based Smart Logistics & Accessibility Platform for Punjab

An AI-powered smart logistics and road accessibility platform designed specifically for Punjab, India, helping commuters, drivers, and logistics operators navigate roads more safely and efficiently.

The platform combines AI, Google Maps, real-time navigation, voice assistance, fuel-station information, road-condition awareness, and driver safety features to provide an intelligent transportation experience.

🗺️ Smart Google Maps Navigation

- Google Maps integration for real-time navigation and route planning.
- AI-based route recommendations considering distance, traffic, road conditions, and travel time.
- Alternative routes during traffic congestion, road closures, or unexpected disruptions.
- Voice-guided navigation with audio alerts for turns, important locations, and road hazards.
- Nearby fuel-pump detection along the selected route.

⛽ Smart Fuel Assistance

- Locate nearby petrol pumps and CNG/fuel stations.
- Show available fuel stations along the journey.
- Provide fuel-stop recommendations based on the estimated journey and distance.
- Display fuel-station working hours wherever reliable information is available.
- Help logistics drivers plan fuel stops efficiently.

🚦 Road & Traffic Intelligence

- Real-time or API-based traffic and road-condition information.
- Alerts about traffic congestion, road closures, accidents, and potential hazards.
- AI-assisted selection of safer and faster routes.
- Location-based alerts for important road conditions.

🔊 Voice & Accessibility Features

- Voice-based navigation and commands.
- Audio notifications for turns, hazards, fuel stations, and important destinations.
- Hands-free interaction to reduce driver distraction.
- Simple and accessible interface for commuters and logistics workers.

🕐 Working Hours & Rest Management

- Track driving/working hours for logistics drivers.
- Recommend appropriate breaks during long journeys.
- Provide nearby rest stops and facilities where available.
- Sleep/rest-cycle reminders to reduce driver fatigue.
- Alert drivers when excessive continuous driving may become unsafe.

🤖 AI-Powered Logistics

- Intelligent route optimization.
- Travel-time and fuel-efficiency recommendations.
- Smart planning for deliveries and multiple destinations.
- AI-based suggestions based on traffic, weather, road conditions, and driver requirements.

🎯 Objective

The goal is to build a Punjab-focused intelligent transportation and logistics platform that makes road travel safer, more accessible, fuel-efficient, and convenient by combining AI, Google Maps, voice assistance, navigation, fuel-station intelligence, and driver rest management in one platform.

Tech Focus: AI/ML • Google Maps API • Route Optimization • Voice Assistance • Geolocation • Traffic Data • Weather API • Fuel-Station Data • Accessibility • Driver Safety • Smart Logistics
